Things Penname may NOT catch on its own. Use this file to practise the
"Penname missed something? Add it here" box on the review screen.

1. Lowercase names in quick notes:
   spoke with jane smith re her legacy pledge

2. Organisations without "Foundation", "Trust" or similar in the name:
   A matched grant from Oxfam and Barclays is expected.

3. Amounts written in words:
   She has promised ten thousand pounds over two years.

4. Bare numbers with no currency sign:
   Gift Aid reference 123456789. Last gift 5000.

5. A surname listed before a first name:
   Smith, Jane
   Okafor, David

These should all be caught (for comparison):
   Dear Margaret Wilson, your gift of £2,500 arrived on 14 March 2025.
   Call 020 7946 0958 or write to margaret@example.org.
