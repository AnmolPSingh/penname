Penname
A free tool for nonprofits
philanthropel.com

Nothing ever leaves this device. Penname has no server, no analytics and no network calls. Your documents are read in memory, the key file is encrypted on this device, and the only thing you ever share is the safe copy — when you choose to paste it.

How it works. Penname finds private details — names, organisations, emails, phones, addresses, gift amounts, dates, IDs and fund codes — and gives each one a stand-in. You check the list, save the safe copy for your AI assistant, then bring the real details back with the encrypted key file.

Please read this part. Penname does not make you compliant and does not make data anonymous. Because the real details can be restored, the safe copy still counts as personal data. What Penname does is reduce how much personal data you share — a recognised safeguard, not a get-out. You are responsible for reviewing the output. A tool, not legal advice.

No tool finds everything. Read your document once more before you share it, and add anything Penname missed on the review screen.
